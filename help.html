What is CraftMander?

CraftMander is a Guild Wars 2 crafting tool that connects to your GW2 account and tells you which recipes you can craft right now based on your current material storage.

You build a personal watchlist of recipes you care about — either by searching manually or loading a curated Library — then check the Dashboard to see your craftability status at a glance.

ℹ️ Your watchlist and API key are saved locally in your browser and persist between sessions. No data is stored on any server.
🔑

Setting Up Your API Key

Dashboard

CraftMander needs a GW2 API key to read your material storage. Here's how to create one:

  1. Go to account.arena.net/applications and log in with your GW2 account.
  2. Click New Key and give it a name (e.g. "CraftMander").
  3. Enable the inventories permission. No other permissions are needed.
  4. Copy the generated key and paste it into the API Key field on the Dashboard.
  5. Click Load Materials. Your account name and material counts will appear.
💡 Your key is saved in your browser's local storage after the first successful load, so you won't need to re-enter it each visit.
⚠️ Only grant the inventories permission. Never share API keys that have account, trading post, or wallet permissions with third-party tools.

Recipe Explorer

Explorer page

The Explorer is where you search for individual recipes and add them to your watchlist. Type any item name in the search box and matching recipes will appear instantly.

Element Description
Search box Filters recipes in real time as you type. Matches anywhere in the item name.
+ Watch button Adds the recipe to your watchlist. Changes to ✔ Added once it's on the list.
Item rarity color Names are colored by rarity — grey (Basic), blue (Fine), green (Masterwork), yellow (Rare), orange (Exotic), pink (Ascended), purple (Legendary).
Watchlist panel Shows all watched recipes. Click ✖ to remove an item. No craftability info is shown here — load materials on the Dashboard for that.
📊

Dashboard

Dashboard page

The Dashboard is the main view for seeing what you can craft. After loading your materials, each watchlist item shows a live craftability badge.

Badge Meaning
✓ Ready You have all the required ingredients in your material storage right now.
✗ Missing One or more ingredients are missing or insufficient.
(no badge) Materials haven't been loaded yet — enter your API key and click Load Materials.
💡 Craftability checks are recursive — if an ingredient can itself be crafted from your materials, CraftMander accounts for that automatically.

Clicking a watchlist item

Clicking any item in the watchlist opens the Recipe Detail panel at the bottom of the page. This shows the full crafting tree with per-ingredient have/need counts, plus quick links to the GW2 Wiki and GW2Efficiency.

📚

Libraries

Libraries page

Libraries are curated collections of related recipes — for example, all recipes required for a specific Faction Provisioner vendor. Instead of searching one by one, you can add or remove an entire collection at once.

  1. Select a collection from the left sidebar.
  2. The middle panel previews all recipes in the collection, with craftability badges if your materials are loaded, and a Watching tag for items already on your watchlist.
  3. Click ＋ Add All to Watchlist to add every recipe in the collection, or ✖ Remove All from Watchlist to remove them all at once.
ℹ️ Collections labeled — TODO are placeholders for future recipe sets that haven't been fully confirmed yet. They won't appear in the sidebar until their recipes are filled in.
📋

Watchlist

Your watchlist persists across all three pages and is saved automatically to your browser's local storage. Items remain on the list until you remove them manually.

Action How
Add a recipe Search for it on the Explorer, or use Add All on the Libraries page.
Remove one recipe Click the ✖ button on any watchlist item.
Clear entire watchlist Click the 🗑 button in the Dashboard watchlist header. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is deleted.
⛓

Serial Mode

Dashboard page

The default craftability check treats each recipe independently — as if your full material stack is available for each item. Serial Mode simulates crafting recipes in order, consuming ingredients as you go.

This answers the question: "If I craft these items one after another, how many can I actually complete before I run out of materials?"

Reading the serial mode indicators

Indicator Meaning
✓ (green) This item is craftable in sequence — materials are still available when its turn comes.
✗ (red, dimmed) Materials were used up by earlier crafts — this item can't be made.
– (grey, faded) Manually excluded from the serial run. Click to re-include.

Craft All in GW2Efficiency

When Serial Mode is active and at least one item is craftable in series, the ⚙ Craft All in GW2Efficiency button appears. It opens the GW2Efficiency crafting calculator pre-loaded with all the items that made the cut, so you can get a full shopping list instantly.

💡 Order matters in Serial Mode — the watchlist is processed top-to-bottom. Items at the top get first pick of shared ingredients. Use the ✖ remove button to reorder by removing and re-adding items in the order you want.
🌿

Crafting Tree

Dashboard page

Clicking a watchlist item on the Dashboard expands the Recipe Detail panel, which includes a full crafting tree showing every ingredient and how many you currently have.

Element Description
▶ toggle button Expand an ingredient's own sub-recipe (if one exists). Click again to collapse. Nests up to 4 levels deep.
have/need count Shows how many of this ingredient you have vs. how many are needed. Green = sufficient, red = insufficient.
🔗

External Links

Each recipe detail panel provides two quick links:

Link What it does
📖 GW2 Wiki Opens the official Guild Wars 2 wiki page for the output item.
⚙ GW2Efficiency Opens the GW2Efficiency crafting calculator pre-loaded with this single item, so you can see a full materials breakdown and where to source missing ingredients.
